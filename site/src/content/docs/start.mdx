A service mesh puts a proxy next to every application. The proxy sees every
request, so it can report metrics and trace spans for applications that
were never instrumented. Kiali turns that telemetry into a live map of your
system.

There is one thing the proxy cannot do: know that the request leaving your
application belongs to the request that entered it a few milliseconds
before. Without that, every hop becomes its own trace. In this tutorial you
see the problem, understand it and fix it in two different ways.

Everything runs on your laptop in a [kind](https://kind.sigs.k8s.io/)
cluster. Nothing leaves your machine.

## How the session goes

<Steps>

1. **At home, before the event.** [Prepare your laptop](/kiali-tracing-tutorial/prepare/).
   `make prepare` creates the cluster and installs everything. Ten to
   fifteen minutes on good wifi.

2. **Hands-on.** Five short steps, left sidebar. Each page says what to
   run, what you should see and why it matters.

</Steps>

## Reading these pages

Commands you type look like this:

```bash
make deploy-apps
```

Every `make` target is a thin wrapper around `kubectl`, `helm` or
`istioctl`. The pages show the real command next to it, and
[Commands](/kiali-tracing-tutorial/reference/commands/) lists them all.

Terminal windows show the real output of each command, captured on the
author's laptop. They play once when they scroll into view and then stay,
so you can read them at your own pace. Your pod names and numbers will
differ.

<Aside type="tip" title="Tabs to keep open">
Kiali: [localhost:20001](http://localhost:20001). Jaeger:
[localhost:16686](http://localhost:16686). Grafana:
[localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000). Prometheus:
[localhost:9090](http://localhost:9090). All four work once `make prepare`
finishes. The shop, [localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080), works from
step 2.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Time ranges">
Kiali shows the last minute by default, Jaeger searches the last hour,
Grafana dashboards the last 5 to 30 minutes. When a view looks empty, check
its time picker before anything else.
</Aside>
